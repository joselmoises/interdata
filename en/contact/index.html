Before you ask

Frequently asked questions

What information should I prepare for a quote?
It helps a great deal to tell us: the site address, the approximate number of network points or cameras, whether the building already has infrastructure (trunking, rack), and your target timeline. A floor plan, even a rough one, speeds things up considerably.
Is the site visit free?
Yes, in Maputo and Pemba. For other provinces we agree travel costs in advance, and they appear separately in the quote.
How soon will I get the proposal?
After the site visit, normally within three working days. Larger projects, or those needing imported equipment, can take longer — in which case we say so at the outset.
Do you issue invoices and take part in public tenders?
Yes. We are a company registered in Mozambique, we issue invoices and we have experience bidding for tenders and supplying the required documentation.

We are a hands-on team of technicians, developers and support specialists, united by a single purpose: keeping your operation online.
