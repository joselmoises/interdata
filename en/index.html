Frequently asked questions

What clients usually ask

Do you work outside Maputo?
Yes. We are based in Maputo and Pemba and deliver projects across the whole country. For work outside those two cities, the quote includes travel and accommodation for the team, shown separately and transparently.
How long does a structured cabling installation take?
It depends on the number of points and the state of the building. An office with 40 to 60 points is normally finished in 5 to 10 working days, including certification. After the site visit we provide a schedule with firm dates.
Is the initial assessment charged?
No. The diagnostic visit and the survey of your current infrastructure are free and carry no obligation. We only start invoicing once the proposal is approved.
Do you provide a warranty and support after installation?
Yes. Every installation carries a workmanship warranty, in addition to the manufacturer warranty on equipment. We also offer maintenance contracts with agreed response times (SLA) and periodic reports.
Do you supply the equipment as well?
We do. We work with brands such as Cisco, Ubiquiti, Hikvision, MikroTik, Dahua and Grandstream. If you prefer to use your own equipment, we integrate it into the project.
