Frequently asked questions

Social Media Management — common questions

How many posts per month?
It depends on the plan and the sector. We would rather post less with something useful to say than post often with nothing. We agree the frequency at the start, based on what can be sustained.
Who replies to customer messages?
We answer general enquiries and pass sales enquiries to your team. We define that boundary in writing at the start.
Do I have to pay for advertising?
It is not compulsory, but organic reach is now very limited. A small, well targeted budget usually achieves more than posting more often.
Do you take the photographs?
We can. We run sessions at your premises — sites, team, products. Your own material is clearly distinguishable from stock images.
How do I know it is working?
From the monthly report, with concrete numbers: messages received, enquiries generated and reach. If the numbers do not move, we change the approach.
Do you work with LinkedIn?
We do. For companies selling to other businesses and bidding for tenders, LinkedIn is usually worth more than Instagram.
