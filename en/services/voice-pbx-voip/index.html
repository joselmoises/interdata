Overview

Calls reach whoever should answer

We provide complete voice solutions for business, including analogue PBX, IP PBX and VOIP systems. The goal is simple: calls reach whoever should answer, without the caller waiting or being transferred three times.

We install and configure the exchange, the extensions, the automated attendant and the call queues, integrated with the national operator and the company network. Where it makes sense, we keep existing analogue lines running in parallel during the transition.

  • Automated attendant and queues routing by department
  • Extensions that work outside the office, on mobile or laptop
  • Reports on answered calls, missed calls and waiting time
  • Fallback routing for when the internet goes down

Frequently asked questions

Voice Solutions — common questions

What is the advantage of IP PBX over analogue?
Lower cost per extension, extensions that work outside the office, automated attendant, queues, recording and reports. Analogue still makes sense for small, stable installations, or as a backup line.
Can I keep my current number?
In most cases yes. We confirm with the operator before making any change and plan the transition so no calls are lost.
Do extensions work outside the office?
They do. With a softphone on a mobile or laptop, staff answer the company extension anywhere with internet.
What if the internet goes down?
We configure fallback routing — for example, diverting calls automatically to mobile numbers. Where continuity is critical we keep an analogue emergency line.
Can calls be recorded?
They can. We only note that recording requires informing the other party, and we configure the automatic notice at the start of the call.
Can I see how many calls were missed?
Yes. The system produces reports on answered calls, missed calls and waiting time, per extension and per queue.
