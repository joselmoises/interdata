Installation of access control, biometric readers, RFID cards, turnstiles and electromagnetic locks. Attendance records and exportable reports.

Overview

Know who came in, where and at what time

Controlling who comes in, where they can go and at what time is one of the security measures with the best return for its cost. We install access control systems using biometrics, cards or codes — from a single reader on one door to multiple entrances with different permission profiles.

Beyond security, it solves an administrative problem: entry and exit records become automatic, with attendance reports you can export straight into payroll.

  • Profiles with authorised hours and doors, per employee
  • Block a user immediately, without changing any locks
  • Attendance reports exportable for payroll
  • Emergency exit always guaranteed by mechanical means

Frequently asked questions

Access Control — common questions

Biometrics or cards — which is better?
Biometrics avoids borrowed or lost cards and suits attendance recording better. Cards are faster in high-traffic areas and work better in dusty environments or where staff wear gloves. In many cases we use both together.
What happens during a power cut?
Installations include backup power, and the emergency exit is always guaranteed by a mechanical button or panic bar, as fire safety requires.
Can I see who came in and when?
Yes. The system keeps an access history per user and per door, with exportable reports.
Can it be used for staff time and attendance?
It can. We configure the attendance reports and help export them in the format your accounts team uses.
Can access be restricted by time of day?
It can. We define profiles with authorised hours and days — for example, allowing warehouse entry only during working hours.
Does it integrate with CCTV?
Yes. Integration links each access event to the camera image from that moment, which is useful in internal investigations.
