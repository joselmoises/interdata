Who we are

A Mozambican company, with its own team on the ground

Interdata is a Mozambican company specialising in technology, computing and network infrastructure solutions, dedicated to supporting companies, government institutions and industry through their digital transformation.

We supply high-quality technology equipment and professional solutions that deliver performance, security and operational efficiency. We work out of Maputo and Pemba, with the capacity to travel anywhere in the country.

  • International standards applied on every job
  • Direct supply of equipment from recognised brands

What guides us

Mission, vision and values

Mission

To support Mozambican organisations through their digital transformation, delivering infrastructure that is reliable, secure and suited to local conditions — with support that does not end at handover.

Vision

To be the national reference in IT infrastructure and electronic security, recognised for the technical quality of our installations and the consistency of our after-sales support.

Values

Technical rigour, transparent pricing, deadlines met and clear communication. No shortcuts — even where nobody will see what goes inside the wall.
